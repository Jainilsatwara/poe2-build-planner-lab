## 🪶 The Philosophy Behind Atlas Forge

Most build planners assume you already know what you want. They ask you to pick a class, pick a skill, and then drag nodes around like you're doing homework. Atlas Forge starts from the opposite end. It asks: *what does your exile keep doing when you're not looking?*

If ninety percent of your playtime is spent hovering over a loot filter decision, the Loot Tracking module will surface that. If your damage graph flatlines at level 42 and only recovers at 58, the Damage Analytics panel will flag that valley and compare it against archetype curves. If your passive tree keeps drifting toward the same three clusters across five different characters, the Passive Atlas will draw that gravitational pull for you in a heat-map.

The result is a toolkit that behaves less like a wizard and more like a mirror. It doesn't tell you how to play. It shows you how you already do.

## 🧩 Feature Breakdown

### 🧠 Build Planning Studio

The Build Planning Studio is the heart of the application — a spread of canvases where you sketch a character from level 1 to the deep endgame without ever committing until you're ready. Each canvas is versioned, so you can fork a build, tweak it, and compare it side-by-side with the original as if you were reviewing two different novels.

- **Multi-variant canvases** — Keep up to twelve parallel build drafts open in a single session.
- **Role tagging** — Label builds as *boss-killer*, *mapper*, *support*, *experiment*, or any custom tag you invent.
- **Stat projections** — Every node you allocate updates a live projection panel showing estimated life, energy shield, resistances, and damage multipliers.
- **Import by seed** — Paste a build seed string from a friend and see it reconstructed node-by-node.
- **Snapshot diffing** — Compare two snapshots and see exactly which nodes changed and how those changes ripple through your projected stats.

### ⚔️ Skill & Damage Analytics

Damage in Exile is a symphony, and most players only ever hear the melody. This module decomposes your character's offense into its constituent instruments: base damage, added damage, multipliers, conversions, critical layers, ailments, and the thousand small modifiers that turn a humble skill into a tyrant.

- **Contribution breakdown** — A per-source list of every modifier and how much it actually contributes.
- **Diminishing returns curves** — See when another 10% increased damage stops mattering.
- **Ailment modeling** — Ignite, bleed, poison, and shock are each simulated independently.
- **Boss-value mode** — Toggle a preset resistance and armor profile for common endgame encounters.
- **Historical overlay** — Plot your damage curve across the last eight sessions.

### 💎 Loot Tracking & Economy Lens

Loot is not noise. Loot is a signal about where you're spending your hours and whether those hours are returning. The Economy Lens watches every pickup decision you log and turns it into a story about your farming habits.

- **Session-based loot ledger** — Log drops manually or via hotkey, tagged by zone and timestamp.
- **Value density heat-map** — See which zones gave you the highest value per minute.
- **Filter regret detection** — If you vendor something you later search for, the tool flags it.
- **Currency flow chart** — A Sankey-style view of how your currency moves between hoarding, crafting, and trading.
- **Stash-tab affinity** — Track which tabs tend to fill first, so you can reorganize intelligently.

### 🌳 Passive Progression Atlas

The passive tree is a map of a thousand small decisions, and most players make the same hundred twice. The Progression Atlas draws your path across builds and surfaces the patterns that hide inside repetition.

- **Path heat-mapping** — Coloring that shows how often your builds route through each cluster.
- **Commit-point warnings** — Nodes you always seem to regret taking are highlighted in amber.
- **Build-archetype overlays** — Overlay recommended paths from curated archetypes.
- **Leveling milestones** — Schedule your tree at levels 10, 25, 45, 70, and beyond.
- **Respec cost estimator** — Project the regret cost of changing course at each level band.

### 🎮 Trainer-Style Practice Modules

Between the analytical tools sits a set of optional practice modules designed to sharpen the reflexes that endgame content demands. These are drills, not overlays — they run alongside your session like a coach calling out reps.

- **Dodge-pattern drills** — Timed prompts for sidestep, roll, and repositioning.
- **Boss-tell flashcards** — Learn the animation signatures of major encounters.
- **Resource discipline timer** — Practice managing flasks and mana without panic.
- **Map-mod literacy quiz** — A rotating quiz of map modifier interactions.
- **Endgame scenario simulator** — Walk through abstracted combat scenarios before you commit a portal.

### 🖥️ Responsive Desktop UI

The interface scales cleanly from a 1366×768 laptop screen to a 4K ultrawide without a single panel collapsing into nonsense. Panels are dockable, floatable, and collapsible. A full density setting lets you choose between *airy* (for planning sessions), *compact* (for dual-monitor work), and *cinematic* (for streaming).

- Adaptive layouts that remember your last arrangement per monitor.
- Dark, dim, and high-contrast themes for long evening sessions.
- Full keyboard-navigable — every panel reachable without a mouse.

### 🌍 Multilingual Support

Localization ships with English, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, and Simplified Chinese out of the box for 2026. Additional community language packs are drop-in compatible.

- Right-to-left layout support prepared for future locales.

- Every UI string lives in a single translation file that the community can expand.

## ⚙️ Configuration & First-Run Walkthrough

The very first time you launch Atlas Forge, a setup wizard walks you through five screens. It takes about ninety seconds if you don't linger.

1. **Locale selection** — choose your UI language and date format.
2. **Profile naming** — give your primary character a label so session history stays organized.
3. **Panel preset** — pick *planner*, *analyst*, *farmer*, or *custom* — each arranges the workspaces differently.
4. **Theme** — dark, dim, or high-contrast.
5. **Sync mode** — choose **manual**, **interval**, or **on-demand**, all of which are described in the next section.

Once complete, you'll land on the Build Planning Studio with a sample build loaded. Delete it whenever you're ready to start fresh — the app will offer to save it as a template first.

## 🔄 Data Sources & How Sync Works

Atlas Forge is built on a **local-first philosophy**. Your build data, loot ledger, and notes live in a human-readable folder structure on your own disk. Nothing leaves your machine unless you explicitly export it.

Three sync modes exist:

- **Manual** — you press F5 or click Refresh to pull in the latest session snapshot.
- **Interval** — a background timer syncs every N minutes (default 15).
- **On-Demand** — sync only fires when you switch to a panel that requires fresh data.

Community-shared datasets — like archetype overlays or boss resistance profiles — are versioned snapshots you can opt into downloading. The app shows a changelog before any dataset is applied, and you can roll back at any time from the history view.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Atlas Forge affect my game client in any way?**
No. It runs as an independent desktop application. It reads only the data you provide to it — manually, or via exports you choose to import.

**Can I use it on multiple machines?**
Yes. Because everything is local-first, you can copy your data folder between machines or keep separate profiles per machine.

**Is there a way to contribute a language pack?**
Absolutely. Every UI string lives in one file, and pull requests adding locales are reviewed within a few days.

**What happens if I stop using the app for a year?**
Your data is plain and portable. Open it in any text editor. No locked formats, no mystery encodings.

**Does it need an internet connection?**
Only for the initial dataset downloads and any opt-in community shares. The core app functions completely offline.

**How often is the boss resistance dataset updated?**
Whenever a game patch meaningfully shifts those values. Every dataset update includes a changelog.
